# Brief: Who Our Customers Are and What Next Quarter Needs

*For the CEO and operating partners. One page. Built on TailSignal's simulated partner network (8,490 households, 2022–2025); the numbers show the method, not the market.*

## What we found

1. **One in five households drives two-thirds of revenue.** "Daycare regulars" bring their dog in about 115 days a year and spend about $6,000 annually. They are 22% of households and 64% of revenue.

2. **There is a ready-made cross-sell.** 671 daycare-regular households never use grooming, though comparable households book about 6 grooming visits a year. Converting one in five is worth about $70,000 a year at our prices.

3. **Our vet partners are missing customers we already serve.** 396 households use our daycares and groomers but never visit a partner vet. Converting one in five adds about $34,000 a year, and gives us the clinical records that make our data more valuable to buyers.

4. **Forecasting cuts staffing cost without turning dogs away.** Staffing daycares from a 13-week forecast would have cost about 7% less than "same as last year." Staffing to a cautious high estimate costs more than it saves, because one extra handler costs more than several missed visits.

5. **Reminders work on average, but we can't yet tell who needs one.** The randomized wellness-plan reminder kept about 17 extra members per 1,000 contacted. Models that try to pick the most persuadable members weren't reliably better than reminding everyone; the pilot is about a third of the size needed to learn that.

6. **Daycare data doesn't predict illness much, yet.** Knowing a pet uses daycare or grooming helps predict vet visits a little, but mostly because those owners behave differently, not because staff spot illness early. Early-warning value appears only for daycare regulars, and only if staff record concerns.

## What we recommend

| Action | Owner | First step |
|---|---|---|
| Grooming offer to daycare regulars | Daycare and grooming partners | Pilot at two sites with a randomized holdout to measure the lift |
| Vet referral program for daycare/grooming-only households | Vet partners | Add a "new patient" offer at daycare check-in |
| Weekly forecast-based staffing | Daycare operations | Use the 13-week plan in the partner portal; review the forecast against actuals monthly |
| Retention focus on daycare regulars | Commercial | Keep reminding all wellness-plan members; expand the randomized pilot to 6,000–9,000 members before targeting |
| Structured "health concern" checkbox at daycare and grooming check-in | Partner operations | Turns staff observations into usable early-warning data |

## How sure are we

- The customer groups are stable: rerunning the analysis on 50 resampled datasets gives the same groups (stability scores 0.76–0.99 on a 0–1 scale).
- Our first segmentation design was unstable and is reported as a failure; the version above was revised and documented before rerunning.
- The forecast is more accurate than last-year-same-week, but its stated uncertainty ranges are too narrow (they catch 64% of outcomes, not 80%). Recalibrating the ranges from past forecast errors brings coverage to 82%.
